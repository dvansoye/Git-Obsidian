# Content

1. **Turning back the clock.** This is the name I have come up with for one of my long-standing goals which the use tools, techniques, and supplements to literally become younger. So far, I have not been firing on all cylinders so to speak. I've had problems with my sleep and other related problems. If [[Zettelkasten/Konzepte/GlyNAC|GlyNAC]] and [[Magnesium]] do the trick, I may at least be back to normal. Back to normal, meaning firing on all cylinders. But my goal is to actually turn back the clock. What does this mean to me? Well, a couple of things come to mind: growing more hair, regaining color in my hair, eliminating age spots on my face, removing age lines on my face, becoming more muscular, becoming trimmer in my abdomen area, running faster and longer, thinking more clearly, having a better memory, being able to have two thoughts at the same time while having a conversation with someone, being able to "track" meaning being able pay attention to someone and having thoughts but holding on to those thoughts until they're done talking. What are the different potential solutions. Supposing GlyNAC and magnesium do the trick, I would look for other things potentially to improve on them such as [[Apigenin]] or [[Magnesium Threonate]]. I'm also thinking about [[Creatine]]. I would also like to investigate Dr Brad Stansfield's YouTube channel. He's a very conservative doctor and he does thorough research and his videos are very clearly explained.
2. **Voyager.** Create a ''voice to voice'' interface to my Obsidian vault, Gemini and (through Gemini) to the internet. Could I build a Python program that talks with me? I want it to take content from my vault, send it to Gemini via the API and then take that and send it to a [[Text to Speech]] API. I would also like it to listen to me, take what I say, use [[Speech Recognition]] to convert my words to text via an API and then respond in a loop. It would basically be a chat interface. I'm thinking of adding this to the cato.md prompt. So, I could have a conversation with [[cato]] about my notes, about research questions that Epimetheus has come up with. There might be a batch component that runs at night pulling together deep research reports that I could discuss with cato. I'm also thinking that if I read a book and have some highlights and reactions from a month ago, cato and I could talk about it, cato could refresh my memory of it, cato could come up with a mnemonic that would help me to remember it in the future. In other words cato would become my co-explorer as we visited a thousand plateaus together. I'm hoping for a leveraged generative relationship. 
3. **Restfulness.** I want to figure out why after taking GlyNAC/Magnesium and feeling so much better, my [[Restfulness]] metric is virtually unchanged. In addition, I still have to get up three or more times at night to urinate. Plus, I still have [[Tinnitus]]. Don't get me wrong. My [[Anxiety]] is gone. My [[Knee Pain]] is gone. My [[Heart Palpitations]] are almost gone. I don't have [[Pruritus]] (itching of my scalp, arms, and chest). OK. I have it a little bit. But it is so much better. But, I would like to figure out restfulness. And right now, it is looking like it is caused by [[UARS]] (labored breathing). I have a few experiments planned including making sure to blow my nose and apply [[Rhinase]] before bed. I also plan to place a pillow under my chin as an alternative to mouth taping. If this doesn't work, I may attempt mouth taping. There are also nasal dilators in my Amazon Shopping cart. I may also ask for an at-home sleep study which is documented [[Physical 2026#Sleep Study|here]].
