- I get up and go to sleep at the same time every day. 
- I eat three meals. For breakfast it is a smoothie with protein. I also drink green tea. I might sneak a few sips of my wife's coffee. I'm drinking green tea because it has less caffeine. This is the only caffeine I have with the exception of a few squares of chocolate after lunch.
- After I have this smoothie, I wait an hour and then exercise.  
- I either run 3 miles at a moderate pace, or I swim for 12 minutes at a moderate pace or I walk for 45 minutes at a moderate pace. I typically jump rope and lift a few weights in the afternoon. 
- I drink a half a beer or a half a glass of wine before dinner.
- I eat dinner at 6:30 am
- I go for a 20-minute walk after dinner.
- I stop looking at screens at 9am. I read on my phone with dark mode on. I know I shouldn't be looking at my phone but I have tested this with my Oura Ring.
- I meditate for 15-20 minutes before bed.
- I go to sleep at 9:30 pm.
